Likely selling range

    An estimate from past ROPA sales, not a formal appraisal. Rounded to the nearest ₱5,000. Actual bids depend on the unit's real condition, documents and demand on the day.

    How the estimate works

    1. It finds sold units like this one: the same brand and model first, then the same brand and body type, then the same body type. Closer variants, years, body type, transmission, condition and more recent sales count more.
    2. It adjusts each of those sold prices to this unit, using what our own sales show about price differences: year model, mileage, condition and transmission.
    3. The fair market value is the middle of those adjusted prices; the likely range covers most of them.
